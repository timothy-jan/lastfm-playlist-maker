Connect Spotify

Add your Spotify app credentials to .env to enable playlist creation.

  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard
  2. Create an app (or open an existing one)
  3. Under Redirect URIs, add:
    http://127.0.0.1:5000/callback
  4. Copy the Client ID and Client Secret into your .env file
  5. Restart the app, then click Connect Spotify
  6. In Development Mode, add your Spotify email under User Management (app owner needs Premium)
